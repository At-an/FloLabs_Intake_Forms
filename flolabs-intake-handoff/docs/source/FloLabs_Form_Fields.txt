** UI/display type **
- Embedded form: Static inline forms injected directly into internal or external web pages.
- Standard page: Dedicated landing page hosted entirely (just like the way HubSpot does). Useful for distributing direct URL links via email.
- Pop-up/Overlay forms to ease form filling or inputing by the user.


** Intake form process **
form Submission (creates application) [tigger: application created] -> applicant receives first the email and is moved to the application pipeline [trigger: applicant declares interest after receiving first email by replying interested, yes, happy to take part or similar words] ->  aplicant receives the second email that contains the link to the first form (contacted); which should be customized as per the job title applied for (meaning an applicant that applied for Social Media intern should see something relevant to his/her role and different from another applicant who applied for NeuroScience Intern role) and the link to the second form (before meeting) [trigger: the applicant has filled and submitted in two forms (contacted and before meeting) before he/she can join orientation meeting] -> Orientation meeting [trigger: orientation meeting complete] -> third form (masterclass) [trigger: applicant has filled and submitted the third form] -> Applicant is Onboarded to the company/team. 


Note: 
1. The applicant must fill the first and second forms (contacted and before meeting) before the orientation meeting.
2. The applicant must fill all the three forms after the application before being on boarded to the comapny/team.
3. The intake form process above serve as a guide to understand the flow of the intern application process. It will  also guide the future automation proposal and implementation. I believe that, it will also shape some of the fields of the tables of the database (when creating or updating any table or the Twenty CRM because of what data you believe should is important to be recorded like the applicantion status, let me know before doing that).
